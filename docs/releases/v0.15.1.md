## Fixed (seen on 2026-10-17)

- **A question answered by message stayed open.** Bernard answered master's "GO d'exécution" with
  `@master oui GO`; master acted on it but the question stayed open, and busmon kept showing a
  decision waiting for 14 hours.
  - master now closes exactly the questions it acts on: `agentbus ask master --withdraw <id>
    --question <qid> --note "answered by message …"`.
  - busmon holds a message to an agent that has an open question once, with a hint: Enter sends it,
    Tab opens that question's panel with your text as the answer.
- **Agents ran older models than roles.toml said, unseen.** On project-b, all five agents still ran
  the models they were launched with three days earlier. `agentbus roles doctor` now shows, for every
  agent, the configured, launched and used model. busmon flags `model≠`, the sentinel checks it every
  day, and master asks you before relaunching with `agentbus swap <role> --back`.

## New

- **Swap ledger:** `agentbus swaps` lists every swap (begin, done, back, failed, retire), who ran it
  and why (`--reason`). busmon flags a role on a fallback profile (`fallback opus`) and its detail says
  how to come back.
- **`agentbus swap <role> --back`** returns a role to its own profile and model. When its provider
  comes back, master gets one reminder.
- **`agentbus retire <role>`** closes one agent's tab (architect once its recommendation is in) with
  the swap's checks. `agentbus shutdown` still stops the whole team.
- **Claude aliases** (`opus`, `sonnet`, `haiku`) in `roles.toml`: a renamed model never breaks a
  launch. An exact id stays possible, to pin one version.
- **master's skill** says when to pop architect (design choices, multi-module specs, a disagreement
  that survived a round, cross-cutting risk), when not to, and to retire it afterwards.
