# v0.13.0 — quotas: stop before the limit, for any AI

Agent teams ran until a provider refused them (`You've hit your session limit · resets 3:45pm`,
Codex's `less than 5% of your 5h limit left`, Kimi's 403), mid-turn. The bus knew Claude's windows
only through a third-party status line's cache, refreshed when the sentinel woke; nobody read the
others. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-quota-awareness-design.md` (rev 2, reviewed by Grok).

## One contract, any AI

Every source produces the same reading: a provider, its windows (used %, reset time, length), when
the numbers were true, and whether it is unknown or unlimited. The policy and busmon know nothing
else, so a new AI needs a reader, not a change to the policy.

- **Claude Code — `agentbus statusline`.** Claude Code hands its `statusLine` command the account's
  `rate_limits` (5 h, 7 d, per-model windows). Declared once per machine
  (`agentbus statusline --print-install` prints the block for `~/.claude/settings.json`; nothing is
  edited for you), it records them in `~/.cache/agentbus/quota-claude.json` and draws the status line
  itself (`Opus 5.5 · myapp · ctx 41% · 5h 12% (08:33) · 7d 54%`); another one can be chained with
  `--then` if you want it. A recorded limit hit in a recent transcript backs it up.
  No ccstatusline, no token, no network.
- **Codex** — its documented app-server request `account/rateLimits/read` (no model turn), cached
  machine-wide for 5 minutes; its session logs as a fallback.
- **Grok** — the billing line of its log (one weekly percent), through a declarative reader, no
  Grok-specific code.
- **Any other AI** — `~/.config/agentbus/quota.toml`: a JSON/JSONL file and its fields, a script that
  prints the reading (its credentials stay in your script), or `unlimited` for a local model.
- `agentbus quota` prints every reading, from a terminal, for anyone.

## The policy (dispatch service)

Every minute the dispatch service reads the sources, publishes them to `{p}:budget`, and:
- at **85%** of a window, sends master one directive (`finish what is in flight, start nothing
  new`) — once per window and reset;
- at **95%**, stops waking the agents of that provider (no prompt, no escalation) until the window
  resets; their directives wait;
- when **master's own provider** is at 95%, pauses the project until the reset (+2 min), with the
  resume directive then. It never replaces a pause set by a person or by master.

A reading that is missing, malformed or older than 30 minutes never warns, holds or releases. Every
write is fenced by the dispatch lease. Thresholds: `AGENT_BUS_QUOTA_WARN`, `AGENT_BUS_QUOTA_HOLD`,
`AGENT_BUS_QUOTA_MAX_AGE`, `AGENT_BUS_QUOTA=off`. Agents now record their client and provider at
launch, which is how the dispatcher knows whose quota each one draws on; an agent launched before
v0.13 is shown but never held until it is launched again.

## busmon

One gauge per provider in the header — `claude 5h 82% ~25m · codex 5h 96% held 03:10 · grok wk 59%`
(the tightest window; `?` when the reading is unknown or aged; `~25m` is an estimate at the current
pace, display only) — and `held` on the agents of a held provider.

## Limits

Sources move only while their tool works; an idle provider ages into unknown. One account per
provider per machine. Codex's app-server is labelled experimental. A hold stops new wakes, not a turn
in progress — the 85% warning exists for that. Kimi and agy have no built-in reader yet: plug them in
through a `command` reader.

## Upgrade

`./setup.sh update`; `agentbus statusline --print-install` and paste the block into Claude Code's
settings; `agentbus --project <p> service restart dispatch`; relaunch agents (or `agentbus up <p>`
for new ones) so they record their provider.
