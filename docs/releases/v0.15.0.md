# v0.15.0 — work resumes after a quota reset, and a long wait has a way out

Spec: `specs/2026-10-16-quota-resume-and-fallback-design.md` (rev 3.1, reviewed
by Codex four times).

## Fixed (seen on project-b, 2026-10-16)

- **A tab blocked by its own client is shown as waiting for you.** After the weekly limit, Claude
  Code showed master a menu ("Stop and wait for limit to reset / …"). busmon said `working`.
  Now a pane the dispatcher sees `blocked` (a limit menu, a permission prompt) reads `waiting`
  with the flag `prompt`. It fades, counts in the header, and rings once per episode. Its detail
  says "Declared working (shown as waiting)" and names the pane to answer in. A declared
  `blocked` or `waiting`, or an open ask, is never covered.
- **An escalation no longer sticks.** master's blocked tab was escalated once, to master itself;
  once the menu was closed, master stayed unwoken with its resume directive unread. An
  escalation caused by the pane's state is now re-armed when the pane is ready again. When the
  escalation target cannot act (it is the stuck agent, its provider is held, its own tab is
  blocked), the escalation is a notify for the person, naming the tab.

## New

- **Resume after a hold.** When a provider's hold ends (released, served again, or reset
  passed), every agent of that provider with an accepted task or a declared `working` turn is
  told to resume it, once per episode, and master gets one notice. The hold ends only once every
  resume is written, so a crash or a failed write is retried and never repeated.
- **The quota pause follows master's provider.** If master runs on a free provider, the pause
  ends with the resume directive. If master's new provider is held too, the pause is replaced
  by one for that provider.
- **A long hold asks for a decision.** A hold due to last more than 2 h
  (`AGENT_BUS_QUOTA_LONG_HOLD`, `0` = off) sends master one directive naming the held agents
  with work in flight; master asks you whether to swap them. When it is master's own provider,
  the notify is for you, and busmon's pause segment adds "long pause, see Activity".
- **Fallback profiles.** In `roles.toml` (schema 2), `fallback_profiles = ["opus"]` on a role:
  each one defined, other than its own profile, on another provider, at most 3. Run
  `agentbus roles fallbacks <role>` to list them; it flags a reviewer that would share coder's
  provider.
- **`agentbus swap <role> --profile <p> [--now] [--force]`** replaces an agent by its role on
  another profile. Every check runs before anything is closed:
  - the profile is allowed and its client is installed;
  - the agent has a live, verified identity, and the caller is not in its pane;
  - it has no open 4-eyes challenge on either side and no mission in progress;
  - it is not running a turn, unless `--now`.

  Then the swap:
  1. writes an intent: the dispatcher leaves that pane alone, and no new request or challenge
     opens on it;
  2. closes the tab and waits for the old process to end;
  3. launches the role on the profile (`agent-spawn … --profile`, where a failed bind is fatal);
  4. proves the replacement: a new pid, a new pane, the profile's client and provider;
  5. sends the agent the resume of its work.

  A failure after the close prints the recovery command. busmon flags `swapping`.

## Not in this release

A durable swap record, a busmon `fallback` flag and an automatic swap back at the reset. The
resume notice reminds master to swap back what it swapped.
