# v0.14.5 — panels you can tell apart

Field feedback: the only difference between the focused panel and the others was a thin line,
blue or grey. Bernard chose, among four proposals, a colour per panel.

## Changed

- **A colour per panel**: Agents blue, Activity green, Board violet, Requests yellow (the theme's
  accent, ok, dispatch and attention colours, in the dark and the light theme alike).
- **The focused panel**: its frame is drawn in its colour, and the name in its title becomes a pill
  (dark text on the panel's colour). In the narrow layouts, the active tab is the pill.
- **The other panels**: their frame is faded towards the background, their title keeps its
  colour, so each zone stays recognisable.
- Status words in titles (`auto-scroll on`) keep their own colour.
- Only colours changed: the frames, their corners and every title's width are the same
  (`TestAlignmentSuite` still checks them). In 16 colours the pill is a reverse video and the
  unfocused frames keep the grey line; without colour, the focused title is bold.
