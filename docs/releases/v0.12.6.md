# v0.12.6 — results reach master

On the project-b team, coder and foureyes finished and approved a document, and said so with
`agentbus report`. master was waiting for a message addressed to it, and a report wakes nobody:
the dispatcher saw nothing owed to master, and the team sat idle with master shown `working`.
Meanwhile master re-armed its subscription every four minutes, re-reading a 524k context each time.

- **`agentbus report <agent> --to <agent>... <message>`** publishes the report and sends each named
  agent a directive: `Report <id> for you (full text: agentbus reports <id>): <first line>`, with
  the report id as its thread. The dispatcher wakes them like any directive.
- **Role prompts** for coder, foureyes, deploy, architect and security now report `--to master`.
  The agent-bus skill states the rule: a report wakes nobody; tell whoever waits.
- **master's role prompt**: with the dispatcher up, master does not re-arm when nothing is in
  flight; it stays armed only while the dispatcher is down or the human may be focused on its
  pane, with a one-hour idle window.

Upgrade: `./setup.sh update`. Running agents keep the prompt they booted with: restart them, or
tell them once.
