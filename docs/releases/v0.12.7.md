# v0.12.7 — what an agent needs from you, at the top

master declared `waiting` with "see my last report". Opening its detail showed that sentence, then
a long unformatted Activity window to scroll through to find the report. And the Requests panel
said `43 waiting`, though none of them was for the person reading it.

- **An agent's detail shows its last report in full**, with its line breaks, right after its
  status message. For an agent `waiting`, the section is titled "What <agent> needs from you" and
  ends with how to answer (`i`, then `@<agent> <your decision>`). If the report is older than the
  Activity window, the detail says so (`agentbus reports`).
- **Requests count what is for you.** The frame reads `1 for you · 42 between agents`: tracked
  requests and untracked directives addressed to you come first; directives between agents that
  were answered by a report or a status rather than a `reply` are still listed, but they are the
  agents' to close. The header counts only yours.
