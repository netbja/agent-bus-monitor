# v0.14.1 — questions you can open, texts you can read

Field feedback on v0.14.0, from the first real ask (project-b, master's two decisions).

## Fixed

- **`!` did nothing in an agent's detail**, and F4 is taken by some desktops (it toggled the
  microphone). The questions now open with `!` or `w` from the panels, and with **Enter**, `!` or
  `w` in the detail of an agent that asks; the detail says so (`enter (or ! or w) to answer`). F4
  still works where the desktop leaves it alone.
- **A report written on one line read as one block.** busmon now gives such a text the structure its
  author typed inline: an upper-case opening sentence becomes its title, each upper-case label that
  opens a sentence (`DECISION 1 -`, `CE QUE FAIT L'OUVERTURE:`, `VERIFIED:`) starts a paragraph in
  bold, and two or more inline choices (`(1) … (2) …`, `(X) … (Y) …`) become a list. Texts with
  line breaks are rendered as Markdown, unchanged.
- **Lines that wrapped to column 0** in the agent detail: the `lag` explanation and the `Gap`
  warning wrap under their value; each Recent activity entry wraps under its text, at most three
  lines (`…`, the whole text is one Enter away in Activity); the thread lines of a message detail
  are cut to the overlay width.
- When an agent has open questions, its detail no longer suggests `i, then @agent <decision>`:
  the questions above say what it needs, and its last report is shown as `Last report`.
