# v0.15.3 — a quota hold that does not flap

## Fixed

- **A quota hold no longer ends and restarts every few minutes.** When the 5h window was at its
  limit and the statusline kept reporting the same 100 %, that reading kept its first observation
  time (an unchanged value is not a new observation), aged past 30 minutes and was set aside; the
  7d window, fresh and low, then released the hold, and the next reading held again. Each turn
  paused and resumed the project for the coordinator's provider, so master received "Project
  resumed" about every ten minutes (seen on ai-tradex-solana, 2026-10-17). A hold now ends only
  when **the window that set it** is read under the threshold, renewed (a new reset) or past its
  reset — use within a window only grows until its reset, so an aged reading of it still stands.
  A call the provider served after an aged reading at 100 % still ends the hold at once (a reset
  granted early, as in v0.14.6).
- **master declares `waiting` only while one of its questions is open.** Once the answers leave
  nothing to do (the team paused, the next step is the person's), it declares `idle`, so busmon
  no longer fades and rings for a decision that is already taken.

## Upgrade

`./setup.sh update`, then `agentbus --project <p> service restart` for each project; master reads
its updated skill (a link into the repository) on its next start.
