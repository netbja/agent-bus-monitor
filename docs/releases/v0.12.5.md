# v0.12.5 — project pause; @all as one line

The project-b team ran out of session quota at 98%. Its resume was scheduled by master
inside its own Claude Code session (a background shell): invisible in busmon, unknown to the
other agents, and lost if the pane closed.

## A project pause

- **`agentbus pause --for 2h | --until 02:15 | --until-reset [--to <agent>] [reason...]`** writes
  `{p}:pause`: until when (from Redis time), why, who paused, who gets the resume. A pause always
  ends: one of the three deadlines is required. `--until-reset` reads the account's session reset
  from `{p}:budget` and adds two minutes. `pause --status` prints it; `agentbus resume [message]`
  ends it early and sends the resume directive at once.
- **While paused, the dispatcher wakes nobody**: no prompt, no escalation. Directives sent in the
  meantime wait in each agent's queue.
- **At the deadline, the dispatcher ends it**: one fenced script checks it still holds the lease,
  that the pause is the one it read and that Redis time is past the deadline, then deletes the
  pause and sends the resume directive (to `master`, or `--to`, or `AGENT_BUS_ESCALATE_TO`). The
  same pass then wakes master to read it, and the queued directives wake their agents.
- **busmon's header** reads `paused · resumes in 1h52 (quota)`, and `paused · resume due` if the
  deadline has passed but no dispatcher has ended it yet.
- **Who pauses:** master, per its skill, when the quota runs out (sentinel reminds it at 90%), or
  you from a terminal. No more sleeps in an agent's session.

## @all

- **`@all` reaches only the agents listed in `{p}:agents`.** The conductor, which only publishes
  reports, appeared in the roster and received `@all` directives.
- **Activity shows a fan-out as one line**, `→ @all (6)`. The bus still carries one entry per agent
  (each is delivered and acknowledged on its own); only the display groups a person's identical
  directives sent within a second. An `@agent` filter still finds the line.

Upgrade: `./setup.sh update`, then `agentbus --project <p> service restart dispatch` (the pause is
the dispatcher's), and reopen busmon. Running agents keep the skill text they booted with.
